I have only one father, I am named for him. 
I am honored. 

During my youngest years he was an enigma. 
Working, travelling, smiling but not always at home.
Yet he physically built the home I grew up in.

When I was very young, he took us across the world, inspiring me, 
giving me access to innovations I didn’t understand. 
He was pioneering in his field.  
Conservative but bold, constant, curious, yet predictable. 

Swimming was my mothers joy and his children surpassed him in everyday pleasures like this.

Through him, we had many vacations to nature, and moments in the sun. I gained access to technology, and hardware ahead of time.

He was lucky to have married my mother, 
a doctor who healed his skin cancer, 
who vaccinated her children at the breakfast table. 
Who loved him and celebrated his strengths while accommodating his flaws. 

He became challenged by his health and then chose to reinvent himself. 

Defending the environment and social progress became his north star. 
He travelled the world under my mothers encouragement. And he embraced it. 


As he grew older, his children grew beyond him in modern technology.
I introduced him to various global communities through digital means.
He found his tribe there. 

From then I had only words of encouragement. 
He had surpassed me once more by standing up for what he believed in.

He died too soon.

I miss my dad.  We both had so much more to learn.
